Ages 13–15
What is vibe coding?

Vibe coding means describing what you want to build in plain English and letting AI write the code for you. You stay in charge — you decide what it looks like, what it does, and who it's for. The AI handles the technical part. Think of it like having a conversation with a very fast builder.

01

Think of your person

Who are you building this for? A friend, a sibling, a parent? Start with them — not the tech.

02

Write your prompt

Describe what you want: who it's for, what it should do, and how it should feel. Be specific.

03

Refine & share

See what appears. Ask for changes. Keep going until it feels right. Then share it with that person.

What a good prompt looks like
Build me an interactive birthday card for my [friend, sister, brother] who is turning [6]. She loves [KPop Demon Hunters — Rumi, Mira and Zoey.] When you click the card, it opens to reveal a birthday message. [Neon purple and pink] theme, [sparkles, magical and exciting.]

💡 Prompt tips

  • Be specific — vague prompts get vague results
  • Describe the feeling, not just the function
  • If you don't like it, ask it to change one thing at a time
  • You can say "make it more fun" or "simpler" — that counts
  • The back-and-forth IS the skill
